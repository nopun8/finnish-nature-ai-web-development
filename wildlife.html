Animals of Finland

Wildlife

Examples of well-known Finnish wildlife and responsible-viewing guidance.

Brown Bear

Finland’s largest wild predator. Never approach or feed bears.

Moose

A large forest animal found across much of Finland.

Reindeer

Strongly associated with northern Finland and may be seen near roads.

Wolf

Wolves occur in parts of Finland but are rarely seen by most visitors.

Lynx

The Eurasian lynx is a shy forest cat.

Saimaa Ringed Seal

A protected seal species found in the Lake Saimaa area.

Responsible Wildlife Viewing

  • Keep a safe distance.
  • Never feed wild animals.
  • Do not disturb nests, dens or resting animals.
  • Keep dogs under control.
  • Use binoculars instead of approaching wildlife.
