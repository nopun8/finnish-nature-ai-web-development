Rivers and Rapids

Oulanka National Park

Oulanka National Park lies in the Kuusamo–Salla area and is famous for river valleys, rapids, forests and the Karhunkierros hiking landscapes.

Nature and Landscape

Oulanka combines boreal forests with steep river valleys, rapids and rocky scenery. The Oulankajoki and Kitkajoki river environments shape many of the park’s most memorable landscapes.

Hiking and Routes

  • Karhunkierros is the best-known long route.
  • Pieni Karhunkierros is a popular shorter circular route.
  • Canyon View Trail and other day routes provide shorter alternatives.
  • Choose routes according to current flooding, ice and trail conditions.

Activities

  • Day hiking
  • Multi-day trekking
  • Nature observation
  • Snowshoeing
  • Paddling on suitable waterways

Facilities and Services

  • Designated camping areas
  • Open wilderness huts and lean-to shelters
  • Day huts and campfire huts
  • Parking areas and trailheads
  • Nearby accommodation and services

How to Get There

Travel is usually easiest by car, but seasonal public transport and local transport services may be available in the Kuusamo area. Check the official Directions page and current Matkahuolto/VR information before travelling.

Safety

  • Spring flooding can cut routes and create mud.
  • Autumn brings shorter daylight and slippery surfaces.
  • Winter stairs and slopes can become icy.
  • Use traction aids and a headlamp in icy/dark seasons.
  • Stay away from dangerous river edges.

Recommended Equipment

  • Hiking boots with strong grip
  • Waterproof shell and warm layers
  • Map and compass
  • Offline route information and power bank
  • First-aid kit
  • Headlamp
  • Traction cleats in icy conditions
  • Food and sufficient water

Seasonal Planning

  • Spring: flood and thaw season; some routes may be inaccessible.
  • Summer: easiest access and busiest season.
  • Autumn: cooler, darker and often slippery.
  • Winter: scenic but requires winter footwear and careful planning.

Rules and Responsible Hiking

  • Camp only where allowed.
  • Use provided fireplaces and obey wildfire warnings.
  • Do not disturb wildlife.
  • Keep pets controlled.
  • Follow current route closures.
