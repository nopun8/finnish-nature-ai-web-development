Eskers and Ice Age Landscapes

Rokua National Park

Rokua National Park is convenient for visitors from Oulu and is known for esker terrain, lichen-covered pine heaths, kettle-hole landscapes and mostly easy-going hiking routes.

Nature and Landscape

Rokua’s landscape is strongly shaped by Ice Age geology. The park features sandy eskers, dry pine forests, lichen heaths and kettle-hole formations in the wider Rokua Geopark landscape.

Hiking and Routes

  • About 35 km of hiking routes are available.
  • Several circular routes suit day trips and longer outings.
  • Most routes are relatively easy-going.
  • Routes do not receive winter hiking maintenance.
  • A demanding accessible route exists at Pitkäjärvi.

Activities

  • Hiking
  • Cycling on permitted routes
  • Skiing and winter recreation
  • Nature observation
  • Geology-focused visits

Facilities and Services

  • Rest spots near routes
  • Accessible facilities at selected locations
  • Nearby accommodation and tourism services
  • Summer transport connections to the Rokua area

How to Get There

During summer schedules, weekday bus connections between Oulu and Vaala can pass through Rokua and stop at local tourism points. In winter, direct public transport may not be available. Some journeys can combine bus and train via Vaala or Utajärvi. Check current timetables.

Safety

  • Dry sandy terrain can be tiring on longer routes.
  • Winter routes are not maintained for hiking.
  • Check daylight and weather.
  • Carry navigation even on easy routes.
  • Prepare for snow and cold in winter.

Recommended Equipment

  • Comfortable hiking shoes
  • Layered clothing
  • Windproof/waterproof shell
  • Map, compass or offline navigation
  • Charged phone
  • First-aid kit
  • Headlamp
  • Water and snacks
  • Winter traction or snow equipment

Seasonal Planning

  • Summer: easiest public transport access.
  • Autumn: cool and colourful.
  • Winter: snow recreation possible but hiking routes are not maintained.
  • Spring: melting snow can make conditions wet.

Rules and Responsible Hiking

  • Use established routes where practical.
  • Keep the area litter-free.
  • Follow fire and camping rules.
  • Respect protected nature and other visitors.
  • Check current destination notices.
