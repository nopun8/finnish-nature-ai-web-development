Fells and Snow-Covered Trees

Riisitunturi National Park

Riisitunturi National Park is known for its fell landscape, wild forest and mire scenery, especially its impressive slope bogs and heavily snow-covered winter trees.

Nature and Landscape

Riisitunturi protects wild forest and mire landscapes characteristic of the Peräpohjola region. Its slope bogs are especially distinctive, and the humid winter climate can create heavily frosted trees.

Hiking and Routes

  • Use marked routes and learn the trail markings before leaving.
  • Winter navigation needs extra care because signs may be covered by frost.
  • Snowshoeing and skiing are allowed, but weather can change rapidly.
  • Check official route descriptions before choosing a hike.

Activities

  • Hiking
  • Snowshoeing
  • Skiing
  • Nature observation
  • Winter cycling only on permitted routes

Facilities and Services

  • Maintained rest spots
  • Lean-to shelters and campfire sites
  • Parking and trail access areas
  • Nearest taxi services are in Tolva

How to Get There

Riisitunturi is remote and public transport is limited. Tolva is the nearest place specifically noted for taxi services. Check official directions and plan return transport in advance.

Safety

  • Wind can erase tracks quickly in winter.
  • Frost can hide marking poles and signs.
  • Some junctions and summit areas are easy to misread.
  • Wind chill makes cold more severe.
  • Carry reliable navigation and emergency equipment.

Recommended Equipment

  • Insulated winter boots
  • Thermal layers
  • Windproof outer layer
  • Map and compass
  • Offline GPS/phone map and power bank
  • Headlamp
  • First-aid kit
  • Emergency snack and hot drink
  • Snowshoes or skis when required

Seasonal Planning

  • Summer: easier navigation.
  • Autumn: cooler and darker.
  • Winter: iconic scenery but demanding navigation and cold.
  • Spring: variable snow and thaw conditions.

Rules and Responsible Hiking

  • Walking, snowshoeing and skiing are generally allowed but zoning rules apply.
  • Camping rules vary by zone.
  • Open fires only at maintained sites where permitted.
  • Cycling is restricted to specified routes.
  • Keep pets on a leash unless an exception applies.
