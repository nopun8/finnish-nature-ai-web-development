Prepare Before You Go

Hiking Equipment

A practical checklist for day hikes and longer trips in changing Finnish conditions.

Clothing

  • Base layer
  • Warm mid-layer
  • Waterproof/windproof shell
  • Hat and gloves

Footwear

  • Hiking shoes or boots
  • Suitable socks
  • Gaiters when needed
  • Traction cleats when icy

Navigation

  • Map
  • Compass
  • Phone/GPS with offline map
  • Power bank

Safety

  • First-aid kit
  • Charged phone
  • Headlamp
  • Emergency whistle

Food & Water

  • Drinking water
  • High-energy snacks
  • Extra food for delays
  • Hot drink in cold conditions

Seasonal Extras

  • Insect protection
  • Sun protection
  • Snowshoes or skis
  • Extra insulation
  • Flotation device for paddling

Important

Equipment needs depend on route length, weather, season and experience.
