About Finnish Nature

Finland offers forests, lakes, mires, rivers, eskers, fells and strong seasonal changes. Northern conditions can vary greatly between summer, autumn, winter and the spring thaw.

Forests

Old-growth forests, pine heaths and spruce-covered hills are important features of northern Finnish nature.

Lakes & Rivers

Lakes, rapids and river valleys shape many popular national-park landscapes.

Four Seasons

Weather, daylight, snow and trail surfaces can change substantially through the year.
