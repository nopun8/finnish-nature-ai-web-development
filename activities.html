Outdoor Planning

Activities & Safety

Outdoor activities can be enjoyable year-round when route, weather and personal ability are considered carefully.

Outdoor Activities

Hiking

Choose a trail that matches fitness, experience, daylight and weather.

Camping

Use permitted camping places and check park rules.

Skiing & Snowshoeing

Winter travel needs warm clothing and navigation skills.

Cycling

Ride only where cycling is permitted.

Fishing

Rules and permits depend on location and method.

Paddling

Use a flotation device and consider water temperature, wind and skill.

Basic Safety Checklist

  • Check weather, route status, daylight and wildfire warnings.
  • Tell someone your route on longer trips.
  • Carry map, compass and offline map.
  • Take enough water and food.
  • Carry first aid, charged phone and power bank.
  • Use a headlamp when daylight may be limited.
  • Turn back if conditions become unsafe.
  • For emergencies in Finland, call 112.
